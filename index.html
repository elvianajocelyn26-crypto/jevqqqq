CHAPTER 01 · THE HISTORICAL PERIOD

The Rebirth
of Beauty

The Renaissance was a period of cultural, artistic, and intellectual revival that began in Italy during the 14th century and gradually spread across Europe.


Inspired by ancient Greek and Roman civilizations, artists explored human anatomy, perspective, classical mythology, nature, and human emotion. Their curiosity changed the way people understood art and the world around them.

CHAPTER 02 · THE MYSTERIOUS PORTRAIT
Mona Lisa · oil on poplar panel

Mona Lisa

LEONARDO DA VINCI · C. 1503–1519

This famous portrait shows a woman with a calm expression and a mysterious, softly smiling face. Leonardo used a technique called sfumato, blending light and shadow so gently that the edges seem to disappear.

Little detail: The sitter is widely identified as Lisa Gherardini, a woman from Florence.
CHAPTER 03 · CLASSICAL MYTHOLOGY
The Birth of Venus · tempera on canvas

The Birth of Venus

SANDRO BOTTICELLI · C. 1485

The painting shows Venus, the Roman goddess of love and beauty, arriving at the shore on a giant shell. Zephyrus and Aura blow her toward land while a woman waits with a cloak.

Little detail: The story comes from classical mythology, which became a favourite source of inspiration for Renaissance artists.
CHAPTER 04 · DIVINE CREATION
The Creation of Adam · Sistine Chapel ceiling

The Creation of Adam

MICHELANGELO · C. 1508–1512

This fresco is part of the Sistine Chapel ceiling. It depicts the biblical moment when God gives life to Adam, with their hands reaching toward each other.

Little detail: The small gap between their fingers has become one of the most recognisable images in Western art.
CHAPTER 05 · KNOWLEDGE & PHILOSOPHY
The School of Athens · fresco

The School of Athens

RAPHAEL · C. 1509–1511

This fresco brings together famous philosophers and thinkers of ancient Greece. At the centre are Plato and Aristotle, surrounded by scholars discussing philosophy, mathematics, and knowledge.

Little detail: Raphael included portraits of other Renaissance artists among the philosophers.
CHAPTER 06 · SYMBOLISM & PORTRAITURE
The Arnolfini Portrait · 1434

The Arnolfini Portrait

JAN VAN EYCK · 1434

This detailed painting shows a man and a woman standing inside a room. It is admired for its realistic objects, rich textures, reflective mirror, and careful use of light.

Little detail: The small convex mirror in the background reflects the room and extra figures.
